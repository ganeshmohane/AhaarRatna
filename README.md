<h1 align="center">🍛 AhaarRatna</h1>

<h3 align="center">Explore the richness of traditional Indian cuisine through a smart, personalized experience 🇮🇳✨</h3>

---

AhaarRatna is a mobile app that celebrates the diverse and vibrant culinary heritage of India. From soulful home-style dishes to festive regional delights, AhaarRatna curates recipes and recommendations tailored to your **mood, age, region, and food preferences** — giving you a true taste of India, plate by plate.
